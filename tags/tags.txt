# IELTS Part 2 Topic Tags
# One tag per line. First tag on every topic line is always the fixed category.
# Normalisation notes follow each group.

## Fixed category tags (always first on every topic line)
people
place
object
experience/activity

## Thematic tags
# --- nature & environment ---
nature              # outdoor, natural settings, wildlife habitats, scenic views, parks, mountains
conservation        # caring for/protecting the environment or animals; eco-minded behaviour

# --- people & social ---
helping_others      # kindness, generosity, volunteering, being helpful to people
influence           # persuasion, encouragement, motivated someone to change behaviour
conflict_resolution # apology, saying sorry, making up after a conflict, forgiveness
advice              # giving or receiving guidance, counselling, recommendations
friendship          # friend, close companion, trust, personal bond
celebrity           # famous person, public figure, well-known personality
admiration          # admire, look up to, inspirational, in awe of someone
family              # family member, relatives, family gathering, family business context

# --- work & career ---
work                # job, career, occupation, part-time work, family business, employment

# --- travel & places ---
travel              # journey, trip, vacation, visiting places, going somewhere, vehicle used for travel
international       # foreign country, abroad, overseas, working/living in another country
navigation          # lost, finding your way, directions, orienteering, getting around
home                # home life, at home, domestic situation, where you live

# --- money & shopping ---
money               # spending, overspending, financial decisions, saving, cost
shopping            # buying items, retail, purchase, customer experience in a shop
service             # good/bad service, hospitality, customer care

# --- learning & intellect ---
self-learning       # self-taught, learned without a teacher, independent study, autodidact
learning            # studying, gaining knowledge, education, acquiring a skill
science             # scientific subject, field of science, biology, robotics, research
reading             # act of reading, books as activity
books               # book as object, written publication
intelligence        # smart, clever, sharp mind, shrewd thinking
problem-solving     # solved a problem, found a solution, clever way out of a difficulty
creativity          # imagination, creative thinking, innovative ideas, inventive approach
planning            # making plans, goal-setting, organised, forward-thinking

# --- technology & media ---
technology          # tech device, gadget, electronic equipment, digital tool (not phone)
app                 # program, software application, digital app, computer/phone program
phone               # mobile phone, smartphone; often in context of rules or dependency
social_media        # social media platforms, internet posts, online content
media               # TV, online programs, streaming, entertainment content

# --- arts & culture ---
art                 # drawing, painting, visual arts, artistic creation, gallery
music               # music, singing, concerts, songs, instruments
movies              # film, movie, cinema, a film you watched
stories             # traditional story, tale, narrative, folklore
culture             # tradition, cultural heritage, customs, folklore background

# --- personal qualities & emotions ---
passion             # loves doing something, deeply interested in, devoted to
talent              # natural gift, innate skill, born ability
self-improvement    # want to improve, develop a skill, get better at something
aspiration          # want to, would like to, dream of, hope to do/have/be something
achievement         # proud of, accomplishment, success, milestone reached
likes_dislikes      # enjoyed, liked, loved, disliked, didn't enjoy — attitude qualifier
happiness           # smiling, joyful, cheerful, positive emotional state
anticipation        # waiting, looking forward to, expecting something special
memorable           # special, noteworthy, stands out in memory, significant moment
sentimental         # emotionally attached, personally meaningful, nostalgic feeling
nostalgia           # childhood memory, fond memory of the past (use with childhood or heirloom)

# --- objects & possessions ---
heirloom            # important old thing kept in family, treasured possession, keepsake
toy                 # toy as physical object; childhood plaything
childhood           # anything specifically framed in the context of being a child/young
everyday_life       # essential, can't live without, daily necessity, dependency
useful              # helpful, practical, beneficial — quality of an object or experience

# --- events & social situations ---
social_event        # event, occasion, gathering, party, group situation
celebration         # joyful gathering, festival, occasion with many smiling people
restriction         # not allowed, banned, prohibited, rules imposed on behaviour
mistake             # accident, error, broke something, careless action, regret
disruption          # unexpected interruption, power cut, inconvenience, things going wrong
first_time          # for the first time, debut, new experience, never done before
adventure           # exciting, thrilling, bold activity, daring new experience

# --- food & body ---
food                # dinner, meal, eating, cuisine, restaurant

# --- other ---
child               # a child or children as the subject (age-specific)
animals             # wild animal, creature, wildlife, fauna
curiosity           # want to know more, eager to learn about, interested in exploring
communication       # language use, talking, expressing oneself, interaction
language            # foreign language, learning/speaking another language
peaceful            # quiet, calm, tranquil — quality of a place or moment
